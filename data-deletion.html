Data Deletion

How to delete your data from any app by Hai Nguyen Le

You can ask us to delete your data at any time. This page explains what data each app may hold, how to delete it yourself, and how to send us a deletion request. It applies to all iOS and Android applications developed by Hai Nguyen Le and is part of our Privacy Policy.

Last updated: September 19, 2026

1. Delete the data stored on your device

Most of our apps keep your content (for example entries, settings and imported files) only on your device. We do not receive a copy, so you can remove it yourself:

  • In the app: where an app offers it, open Settings and choose the option to erase or reset your data.
  • Android: Settings → Apps → [App Name] → Storage → Clear data, or uninstall the app.
  • iOS: press and hold the app icon → Remove App → Delete App.
Uninstalling an app removes the data it stored on your device. If you made a backup file yourself, it stays where you saved it until you delete it.

2. Ask us to delete data we hold

If an app created an account for you, or if you want data linked to your purchases removed from our service providers, send a request by email:

  1. Write to haithangtho@gmail.com with the subject "Data deletion request: [App Name]".
  2. Send it from the email address you used for the account, or tell us that address, and name the app and platform (Android or iOS).
  3. We may reply to confirm that the account is yours before deleting anything.
  4. We delete the data within 30 days of confirming your request and reply when it is done.

4. What we may keep

We may keep limited information if the law requires it (for example records of a paid transaction), or where it is needed to resolve a dispute or prevent fraud. We keep only what is required and for as long as required.
